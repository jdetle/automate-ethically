Every number published on the site lives here: claim → source → date checked.
Policy (from the 2026-08-29 adversarial review):

- A citation must appear adjacent to its claim in the markup. A number without
  a link does not ship.
- Recheck every fact within 6 months of "last checked" or remove it.
- A fact found wrong, materially superseded, or source-dead is corrected or
  removed within 48 hours of discovery.
